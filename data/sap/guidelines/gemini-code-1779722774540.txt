INTENDED USE
This document provides structured, multi-tier criteria for evaluating incoming
resumes for all SAP positions. The system automatically forks into specific 
scoring matrix tracks depending on the candidate's declared total industry 
experience. All scoring is advisory — final hiring decisions remain with 
the hiring panel.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: AUTOMATED TRACK ROUTING & DECISION BANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------

The system scans the profile text for total professional, non-academic experience:
 ➔ Total Experience < 2 Years   👉 ROUTE TO TRACK B (FRESHER / INTERN)
 ➔ Total Experience >= 2 Years  👉 ROUTE TO TRACK A (EXPERIENCED PROFESSIONAL)

FINAL DECISION BANDS BY TRACK:

TRACK A (EXPERIENCED: MID / SENIOR / ARCHITECT)
  85 – 100  →  STRONG HIRE (Fast-track to Technical Interview)
  70 – 84   →  HIRE (Proceed to Stage 2 Online Assessment)
  55 – 69   →  MAYBE (Refer to Senior Reviewer for portfolio audit)
  0  – 54   →  REJECT

TRACK B (FRESHER / ENTRY-LEVEL / INTERN)
  75 – 100  →  STRONG HIRE (Fast-track to foundational interview)
  55 – 74   →  HIRE (Proceed to foundational coding test)
  40 – 54   →  POTENTIAL CONSIDERATION (Good fundamentals; needs training)
  0  – 39   →  REJECT

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: TRACK A — EXPERIENCED PROFESSIONAL MATRIX (>= 2 Years)
----------------------------------------------------------------

DIMENSION 1: TECHNICAL SKILLS & STACK (Max 25 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1A. Advanced Backend & Core ABAP (0–10 pts)
  * 10 pts : Expert Object-Oriented ABAP (OOPs); mastery of modern enhancement frameworks (explicit/implicit BAdIs); high-throughput database interactions; custom transport handling packages; clear use of Eclipse ADT environment.
  * 7–9 pts: Strong in Object-Oriented ABAP syntax; comfortable building robust background jobs, custom BAPIs, and user exits with minimal oversight.
  * 4–6 pts: Moderate backend depth; heavily reliant on legacy procedural syntax (subroutines, forms, macros) with minimal object-oriented usage.
  * 0–3 pts: Superficial list of keywords without supporting project details.

1B. Native HANA & Code Push-Down (0–8 pts)
  * 8 pts : Expert implementation of Core Data Services (CDS) Views, ABAP Managed Database Procedures (AMDP), SQL scripting, table functions, and calculation views; deep understanding of code push-down paradigms.
  * 5–7 pts: Good custom CDS views creation and AMDP exposure; understands code-to-data optimization strategies.
  * 2–4 pts: Limited to basic database views or simple select statements; theoretical-only S/4HANA environment exposure.
  * 0–1 pts: No meaningful HANA or database layer customization experience.

1C. Front-End, UX & Middleware Integration (0–7 pts)
  * 7 pts : Full mastery of SAPUI5 frameworks, JavaScript layouts, custom OData service provisioning (SEGW), extension implementations, and secure Fiori Launchpad setups.
  * 4–6 pts: Strong OData configuration capability; can modify standard Fiori components using extension frameworks or controller hooks.
  * 0–3 pts: Front-end limited to legacy Web Dynpro / SAP GUI, or minimal modern web web protocol exposure.
  * BONUS (+1 to +3): Practical deployment of RAP, CAP, or deep side-by-side configurations inside SAP BTP.

DIMENSION 2: WORK EXPERIENCE QUALITY (Max 25 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2A. Experience Relevance (0–10 pts)
  * 10 pts : 5+ years directly as an SAP Technical Developer or Full-Stack SAP Engineer at a high-growth product ecosystem, premier technology consulting group, or global production cluster.
  * 7–9 pts: 3–5 years directly relevant technical developer experience; or longer tenure with recent S/4HANA environments shift.
  * 4–6 pts: 2–3 years technical experience; rest spent heavily in non-coding technical lines or systems monitoring tasks.
  * 0–3 pts: SAP adjacent positions (functional support helpdesks, basis monitoring) with limited custom coding responsibilities.

2B. Impact & System Outcomes (0–8 pts)
  * 8 pts : Multiple clearly quantified performance and business metrics (e.g., query execution latency reduction, processing speed improvements, batch window contractions).
  * 5–7 pts: Clear link to business value or delivery optimizations across at least 1–2 production implementation runs.
  * 0–4 pts: Vague descriptions ("assisted with system migration", "maintained custom code blocks") without explicit numbers.

2C. Progression & Scope (0–7 pts)
  * 7 pts : Clear technical ownership growth (Developer → Senior Consultant → Track Lead Architect); proven track record managing end-to-end transport release streams.
  * 4–6 pts: Consistent career progression; clear stabilization and accountability patterns.
  * 0–3 pts: Lateral movements without technical expansion; job-hopping patterns without structural validation.

DIMENSION 3: EDUCATION & CREDENTIALS (Max 15 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3A. Degree Level & Institution (0–6 pts)
  * 6 pts : M.Tech / M.S. / B.Tech in CS, IT, or related quantitative fields from top-tier institutions (IITs, NITs, BITS, or international equivalent).
  * 5 pts : MCA or B.Tech / B.E. from reputable regional universities with high technical standings.
  * 0–4 pts: Quantitative, science, or non-traditional technical tracks heavily compensated by extensive workspace metrics.

3B. Academic Performance (0–5 pts)
  * 5 pts : CGPA ≥ 9.0/10 (or equivalent); academic distinctions or scholarship honors.
  * 3–4 pts: CGPA 8.0–8.9 or strong verifiable rank within the graduation technical band.
  * 0–2 pts: CGPA < 8.0 or not declared in the profile layout.

3C. Degree Relevance (0–4 pts)
  * 4 pts : Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or Data Systems specialization.
  * 2–3 pts: Allied engineering track (ECE, EEE) with logical design coursework.
  * 0–1 pts: Completely unrelated field with no core system, programming, or data components.

DIMENSION 4: PROJECTS & SYSTEM PORTFOLIO (Max 15 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4A. Implementation Depth & Architecture (0–7 pts)
  * 7 pts : Full execution of multiple end-to-end enterprise developments (custom interface → middleware OData layer → high-performance AMDP backend data structure); handle complex distributed system tracking.
  * 4–6 pts: 2–3 solid custom systems configurations featuring clear performance tracing metrics and implementation ownership.
  * 0–3 pts: Minor enhancements only; standard report programs with no advanced database tuning or architectural changes.

4B. Integration & Modern Tooling Evidence (0–4 pts)
  * 4 pts : Demonstrated integration tracking across heterogeneous landscapes (e.g., Node.js apps to SAP, cloud connections, web services via JSON/REST pipelines).
  * 2–3 pts: Good standard integration experience (IDocs, RFCs, BAPIs) across legacy architectures.
  * 0–1 pts: Basic inbound file integrations only or no multi-system footprint.

4C. Version Tracking & DevOps Primitives (0–4 pts)
  * 4 pts : Clear usage of modern delivery tools like Git version control frameworks via abapGit deployments; rigorous automated pipeline testing practices.
  * 2–3 pts: Standardized testing tracking configurations using ABAP Unit or automated Test Cockpits.
  * 0–1 pts: No version control or modern automated testing metrics listed.

DIMENSION 5: EXTENSIONS, BTP & CLOUD APPLICATIONS (Max 10 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5A. Cloud-Native Architectures (0–7 pts)
  * 7 pts : Proven production delivery of side-by-side configurations on SAP BTP using CAP setups, Business Application Studio, Node.js, or cloud runtime paradigms.
  * 4–6 pts: Strong working configuration setups across BTP layers; clean cloud connector configurations managing hybrid data syncs.
  * 0–3 pts: Basic theoretical understanding of BTP services without active production building metrics.

5B. Modern SAP Development Frameworks (0–3 pts)
  * 3 pts : Active use of RAP models, custom analytical privileges setups, or advanced Core Data Services configurations.
  * 0–2 pts: Limited to traditional classic ABAP workbench configurations.

DIMENSION 6 & 7: COMMUNITY & PRESENTATION (Max 5 points each)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  * Evaluate based on active technical blogging, Inside Tracks, github code-sharing, and flawless quantified resume structure layout patterns.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: TRACK B — FRESHER / ENTRY-LEVEL MATRIX (< 2 Years)
----------------------------------------------------------------

CRITICAL POLICY: DO NOT penalize Track B candidates for a lack of multi-year enterprise production environments, architectural leadership roles, BTP cloud extensions, or system profiling tools. Re-allocate metrics to evaluate engineering potential, logical core mechanics, and self-taught drive.

DIMENSION 1: CORE LOGIC & CORE PROGRAMMING MECHANICS (Max 25 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evaluates raw programmatic potential. Look for university labs, competitive coding, or basic language constructs.
  * 21–25 pts: Strong foundational grasp of Object-Oriented paradigms (Inheritance, Polymorphism) or clear procedural constructs in C, Python, Java, or fundamental ABAP. Understands foundational database structures (relational schemas, basic SQL queries, multi-table INNER JOINs, selection conditions).
  * 15–20 pts: Good baseline understanding of loops, conditionals, and standard array operations; limited exposure to deep relational database mechanics.
  * 0–14 pts: Superficial syntax awareness; cannot explain code structural flow or basic query retrieval operations.

DIMENSION 2: ACADEMIC & INDEPENDENT PROJECT PORTFOLIO (Max 25 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evaluates hands-on implementation depth on a smaller scale. Look for Capstone, final-year, or self-directed web app builds.
  * 21–25 pts: Completed a comprehensive, functional final year project or independent development application (e.g., Inventory Management Web App, E-Commerce clone, or deep logic scripts). Shows clear execution parameters handling database integration, input form validations, and structured data rendering.
  * 15–20 pts: Standard boilerplate or tutorial follow-along projects (e.g., generic calculator, simple static UI page) showing little custom variation or unique execution.
  * 0–14 pts: No visible project files or purely theoretical documentation listings without verifiable code assets.

DIMENSION 3: TECH ADAPTABILITY & SPECIALIZED SAP TRAINING (Max 20 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evaluates intent to enter the specialized ecosystem.
  * 16–20 pts: Completed structured industry-oriented training programs (e.g., 3-to-6 month intensive ABAP/ERP tracks at NIIT or similar institutes) OR completed verified self-paced certifications via SAP Learning Hub / openSAP. Clear target preparation for baseline developer certification associate tracks.
  * 10–15 pts: Basic introduction courses completed without formal assessment benchmarks or structured training pipelines.
  * 0–9   pts: No visible efforts towards specialized technology domain upskilling.

DIMENSION 4: ACADEMIC CREDENTIALS & DEGREE RELEVANCE (Max 15 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  * 13–15 pts: B.Tech / B.E. / M.Tech / MCA in Computer Science, IT, or Software Engineering with strong academic standing (CGPA ≥ 7.5/10 or equivalent) from a reputable regional engineering institute.
  * 9–12  pts: Allied quantitative engineering track (ECE, EEE, Mechanical) with reliable academic marks, or CS track with CGPA < 7.5.
  * 0–8   pts: Non-technical degree with weak logical foundation profiles.

DIMENSION 5: INTERNSHIPS & PROFESSIONAL EXPOSURE (Max 10 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  * 9–10 pts: Completed a 2-to-6 month technical internship in any technology landscape, actively assisting senior engineers in real workspace environments, support tickets, or prototype builds.
  * 5–8  pts: Completed short industrial exposure stints, company-sponsored workshops, or simulated team-based project configurations.
  * 0–4  pts: No professional workspace exposure or internship history.

DIMENSION 6 & 7: TOOL COGNIZANCE & PRESENTATION (Max 5 points each)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  * Evaluates baseline tooling familiarity (e.g., basic Git operations, GitHub exposure, or SQL editors) and clean, layout-scannable resume readability formats without buzzword-stuffing.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: HARD DISQUALIFIERS (BY-PASS FILTERS)
----------------------------------------------------------------

□ Clear structural misrepresentation (fake certificates or non-existent degrees).
□ Purely point-and-click functional spreadsheet usage without any programmatic, logical scripting, or codebase familiarity across any language layer.
□ For Track A Only: Falling below 3 years of engineering experience *unless* offset by high-value, deep-tier modern technical metrics (e.g., RAP/BTP custom contributions).
