If you love asking "why" and building systems that help others ask "why," you'll thrive here.

----------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------
We are hiring an Associate Data Analyst to join our growing analytics team. You will work closely with senior analysts, product managers, and data engineers to translate entry-level business questions into structured analytical frameworks and convert findings into insights. You'll help maintain dashboards, execute baseline metrics analysis, learn experimental design, and communicate findings that influence day-to-day operations.

This is an excellent launchpad role for a technical graduate with strong logical foundations, a deep commercial curiosity, and excellent communicative skills.

----------------------------------------------------------------
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Data Analysis & Insights
- Extract, clean, and manipulate data from structured relational databases using SQL and Python/R to support ad-hoc business inquiries.
- Help perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) and root-cause analysis on core business metrics under the guidance of senior team members.
- Utilize foundational statistical techniques (descriptive statistics, distributions, and baseline regressions) to investigate customer datasets.
- Support the analysis of running A/B tests and other live digital experiments.
- Document and compile technical findings into clean, readable presentation slides for internal team evaluations.

Reporting & Dashboarding
- Help update, modify, and maintain existing team dashboards and reporting views using enterprise BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, or Looker).
- Track down, verify, and clean data inconsistencies or bugs within reporting surfaces to ensure reliable metric delivery.
- Assist in compiling explicit data documentation for metric stores and definitions.

Data Management & Collaboration
- Write structured, cleanly commented SQL scripts and basic transformation queries.
- Collaborate with data engineers to understand raw ingestion streams and pipeline tables.
- Coordinate with product teams to build basic post-launch tracking matrices for new features.
- Actively maintain team data dictionaries, wiki assets, and internal project knowledge repositories.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Education
- B.Tech / B.E. / B.Sc. / B.Com (Hons) / BBA / B.Stat / B.Math or equivalent in a quantitative, engineering, or business-centric specialization.
- Strong academic record required (CGPA ≥ 7.5/10 or equivalent regional classification).

Technical Skills (Must Have)
- Solid SQL Fundamentals: Comfortable utilizing GROUP BY, HAVING, subqueries, relational database mechanics, and multi-table INNER/LEFT JOINs.
- Scripting Basics: Understanding of Python or R for introductory data manipulation (Pandas dataframes, NumPy operations, or tidyverse methods).
- Data Visualization: Core understanding of visual rendering via spreadsheets, basic BI tools, or plotting libraries (Matplotlib, Seaborn, ggplot2).
- Quantitative Foundations: Good grasp of basic statistical concepts (mean, median, standard deviation, and hypothesis testing patterns).
- Proficiency with Excel / Google Sheets (VLOOKUPs, pivot tables, and conditional formatting).

Experience
- 0–2 years of operational exposure via data internships, university project frameworks, or dedicated technical bootcamps.
- Verifiable proof of independent project work or custom code repositories.
