ROLE OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------
We are looking for a skilled and passionate AI/ML Engineer to join our growing ML team. You will work on some of the most exciting challenges in applied machine learning — from building LLM-powered intelligent features to deploying real-time inference systems at scale. You'll partner closely with product, data engineering, and platform teams to bring ML features to production.

This role sits at the intersection of research and engineering: you need to think rigorously about models and also care deeply about production quality.

----------------------------------------------------------------
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Model Development & Research
- Design, prototype, and evaluate ML/DL models for business problems across NLP, recommendation, forecasting, and/or computer vision domains.
- Stay current with the latest ML research (papers, benchmarks, open-source releases) and identify opportunities to apply state-of-the-art techniques.
- Conduct rigorous offline evaluation using appropriate metrics; design and interpret A/B and online experiments.
- Fine-tune pre-trained foundation models (LLMs, ViTs, etc.) for domain-specific tasks using techniques like LoRA, QLoRA, adapter tuning, and RLHF/RLAIF.
- Build and maintain RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines and agentic AI workflows.

ML Engineering & Production
- Build scalable, maintainable, production-ready ML pipelines from data ingestion through model serving.
- Optimize models for low-latency, high-throughput inference (quantization, distillation, batching, TensorRT, ONNX).
- Develop and maintain REST/gRPC APIs for real-time model serving using FastAPI, BentoML, or Triton Inference Server.
- Implement automated model monitoring for data drift, concept drift, and performance degradation.
- Write clean, well-tested Python code; participate in peer code reviews.

MLOps & Infrastructure
- Manage the full ML lifecycle using tools such as MLflow, Weights & Biases, DVC, and Kubeflow/Vertex AI Pipelines.
- Containerize and deploy models using Docker and Kubernetes on AWS/GCP/Azure.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated model training, evaluation, and deployment.
- Collaborate with data engineers to design reliable feature pipelines and feature stores.

Collaboration & Communication
- Work cross-functionally with product managers, data engineers, and software engineers to deliver ML-powered features.
- Translate complex model behaviour and limitations into clear communication for non-technical stakeholders.
- Mentor junior engineers; contribute to internal ML documentation, best practices, and knowledge sharing.
- Participate in research reading groups and internal tech talks.
